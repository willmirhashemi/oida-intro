**Voice settings**
- Calm, low and documentary, about 140–150 words a minute.
- Stability 0.5–0.6, style low.

**Pronunciation and retakes**
- Set one pronunciation-dictionary alias for "Oida" so every take matches.
- Regenerate a take if "contractors" is stressed wrongly, take 3 sounds like a flat list, or takes 11 and 12 rush.

**Gaps between takes**
- 2 s after take 2, for the stat.
- About 4 s between takes 4 and 5, for the quotes.
- 1.5 s between takes 11 and 12, for the stamp.

## Never say or show
- **"Guaranteed admissible", "court-proof", "tamper-proof", or footage that "proves" something.** We claim tamper-evidence only.
- **When clips are sealed.**
- **Safety-system claims:** "keeps workers safe", "prevents accidents", live alerts.
- **Scoring, ranking, or timing workers.**
- **"No GPS" or "nothing tracks where you go".** Say "no location tracking".
- **Specs stated as finished fact.** Keep the CONCEPT DESIGN super.
- **Hardware detail:** part numbers, chip markings, gram weights, "V2", or a close-up of the latch before the patent is filed.
- **The sales channel:** insurer logos, claim forms, "premiums".
- **The data line:** "data", "dataset", "hours captured", archive views.
- **Audio:** waveforms or mic icons.
- **Unreleased people or sites:** real faces or job footage without a signed release.
- **Any color besides cream, ink and the two grays.**
